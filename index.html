Escalation Pathways

Three types of escalation — Safety, Commercial, and Delivery — each with a defined trigger, path, and resolution timeframe. No ambiguity about where hard problems go.

🔴 Type 1 — Safety
Safety & Regulatory Escalation
Triggered by: safety incident; ONRSR non-approval; union stop-work action; security breach; emergency response failure; public safety risk identified during readiness review.
Trigger: Safety Lead or Security Manager identifies risk
↓ Immediate (same hour)
Program Director notified · Escalation Stand-Up called
↓ Within 4 hours
CEO + COO briefed · ONRSR / Emergency Services contacted if required
↓ Within 24 hours
Steering Committee emergency session · Board notified if public safety
Resolution window: 24–48 hours · No event risk is parked
🟠 Type 2 — Commercial
Budget & Sponsorship Escalation
Triggered by: budget overrun >10%; sponsorship shortfall >$50k; procurement approval needed >$50k; TRAM WM commercial dispute (brand, broadcast rights, equipment costs).
Trigger: Finance Lead or Marketing Lead identifies variance
↓ Within 48 hours
Program Director + Finance Lead review · Options paper prepared
↓ Next SteerCo (or emergency if critical)
Steering Committee decision · Budget reallocation or contingency drawdown
↓ If >$500k or scope change
Board approval required · CEO and CFO present options
Resolution window: 5–10 business days · SteerCo empowered up to $500k
🔵 Type 3 — Delivery
Milestone & Schedule Escalation
Triggered by: missed milestone; critical path task slipping >2 weeks; cross-workstream dependency failing; TRAM WM not delivering on shared obligations; Go/No-Go risk at readiness review.
Trigger: Workstream lead flags in Fortnightly WG
↓ Same meeting
Program Director arbitrates · Recovery plan agreed in 5 days
↓ If unresolved in 5 days
Escalation Stand-Up · Program Director + affected leads + CEO (if milestone)
↓ If critical path at risk
Steering Committee emergency session · Scope / resource decision made
Resolution window: 5–10 business days · No milestone parked without a plan
